Built-in Quick-Start Guide

1. 100% Offline & Private Storage

All 16 stores live locally in your browser's IndexedDB — zero cloud servers or subscriptions. Stick to one browser (avoid Private/Incognito windows) and download a JSON Backup regularly.

2. Single-Source Payments (Zero Double-Counting)

Log deposits and installments via Record Payment. Both your Vendor balance and Budget Line paid totals update automatically from the same payment ledger.

3. Smart Per-Event Currency & Multi-Event Switcher

Manage unlimited events from the top bar. Each event has its own ISO currency with optional manual exchange-rate conversion.

4. Visual Seating & Event Day Mode

Drag tables anywhere on the floorplan, double-click to seat guests (zero double-seating), and tap Event Day for your distraction-free live Run-of-Show, check-in lookup & 1-tap contacts.

5. Keyboard Shortcuts & Undo

Press / anywhere for Global Search, Esc to close modals, and use the 6-Second Undo bar to reverse deletions or bulk updates instantly.
